# 📊 Data Analysis with Excel | Level 3 Data Technician

**Leep Talent Skills Bootcamp · Week 1 Project**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates my developing data analysis skills using **Microsoft Excel**, as part of the Level 3 Data Technician Skills Bootcamp with Leep Talent.

Through practical, hands-on exercises, I worked with retail sales, student performance, bike sales and product sales datasets to organise data, apply formulas, summarise results and identify trends to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean, organise and manipulate datasets using Excel.
- Apply formulas to calculate metrics and categorise data.
- Summarise sales performance using PivotTables.
- Use charts and visualisation techniques to communicate findings.
- Recognise the importance of data protection and responsible data handling.

## 📈 Analysis and Key Insights

**1. Retail Sales Analysis**
- Calculated total and average commission using Excel formulas.
- Sorted and filtered transaction records to examine data efficiently.

**2. Student Performance Analysis**
- Compared English, Maths and Science scores.
- Calculated average and highest scores to identify top performers.
- Applied conditional formatting to highlight high and low results.

**3. Bike Sales Analysis**
- Created PivotTables to compare profitability across countries, age groups and gender.
- Identified the United States as the highest-profit market in the exercise, with **$60,370 total profit**.
- Compared customer segments to identify differences in sales performance.

**4. Product Sales Analysis**
- Summarised product sales by county using PivotTables.
- Applied the `SWITCH` function to classify sales volumes into High, Medium and Low categories.

## 🔐 Data Protection and Governance

Explored the principles behind UK data protection and information governance, including the Data Protection Act 2018, GDPR, Freedom of Information Act and Computer Misuse Act.

Focused on privacy, data minimisation, authorised access and responsible handling of customer information.

## 💡 Key Learning Outcomes

- Developed practical Excel skills for data preparation, analysis and reporting.
- Practised turning raw data into structured summaries and meaningful insights.
- Strengthened understanding of how formulas, PivotTables and visualisations support business decisions.
